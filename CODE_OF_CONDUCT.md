# JoJodle Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of JoJodle, we pledge to respect this Code of Conduct in all community spaces and interactions, both online and in person. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Our Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy towards other community members. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and will not be tolerated:

- Harassing language or imagery.
- Personal insults, hate speech, or derogatory remarks.
- Public or private harassment of any individual.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. Consequences may include:

1. **Warning**: A private reminder of the Code of Conduct.  
2. **Temporary Ban**: Loss of access to community spaces for a set time.  
3. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from all community spaces.

Leaders will follow the Contributor Covenant’s Community Impact Guidelines when determining actions. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
